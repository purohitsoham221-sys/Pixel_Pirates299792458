
## 📌 Overview

**Jan Prashasan Setu** is a Smart Public Grievance Prioritization & Routing System designed to make the process of submitting and managing public grievances easier, faster, and more transparent.

Citizens can submit complaints or grievances to the appropriate government departments, track the status of their complaints, and provide feedback after resolution.

The system also helps government officers efficiently manage, prioritize, and route grievances to the appropriate departments.

---

## ❗ Problem Statement

The traditional manual grievance-handling process can be:

- Slow and time-consuming
- Difficult for citizens to track
- Inefficient for government officers
- Lacking in transparency
- Difficult to manage when there are large numbers of complaints

**Jan Prashasan Setu** aims to address these challenges through a digital and smart grievance management system.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Make grievance submission easier and more accessible.
- Allow citizens to track their complaints online.
- Help officers manage grievances efficiently.
- Improve transparency in the grievance-resolution process.
- Enable better routing of complaints to the appropriate departments.
- Maintain a database of previous complaints and responses.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 👤 Citizen Features

- 🎙️ **Voice-to-Text Multi-Language Input**
  - Citizens can describe their grievances using voice input.
  - Supports multiple languages for easier accessibility.

- 📸 **Photo Upload with Geotag**
  - Citizens can upload photos related to their complaint.
  - Location information can be attached using geotagging.

- 📝 **Submit Grievance**
  - Citizens can submit complaints directly through the platform.

- 🔍 **Track Complaint**
  - Citizens can monitor the current status of their grievances.

- ⭐ **Feedback System**
  - Citizens can provide feedback after their grievance has been resolved.

### 🏛️ Government / Officer Features

- 📂 View submitted grievances.
- 🏢 Assign complaints to appropriate departments.
- 🔄 Update grievance status.
- ⚡ Prioritize and route grievances efficiently.
- 🗃️ Access previous complaints and responses.
- 📊 Improve grievance management and transparency.
